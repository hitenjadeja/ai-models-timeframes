A chronological guide to major AI model releases

This open timeline makes the recent history of artificial intelligence easier to explore. It currently contains 599 notable releases dated from January 2020 through November 2026, including large language models, reasoning models, multimodal systems, coding models, image and video generators, speech models and other foundation models.

Use the provider filters above to compare release activity across organisations, then choose a month to examine a particular period. The open-source licence filter covers releases recorded with a permissive licence such as Apache 2.0 or MIT. Open weights also includes downloadable models with restricted licences. Each timeline entry includes a release date and a concise description of the model or family.

How the timeline is compiled

Release dates and model details are compiled from public announcements, official documentation and provider release notes. The dataset is updated regularly as important new models are announced. Corrections and well-sourced additions are welcome in the public GitHub repository.

For machine-readable context, see the concise AI-system guide, the XML sitemap, and the embedded Dataset structured data in this page.

Frequently asked questions

What is the AI Models Timeline?

It is a free interactive chronology of notable AI and large language model releases from 2020 through 2026, grouped by provider.

Which AI companies and model families are included?

The timeline covers models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta, Mistral AI, xAI, DeepSeek, Alibaba, Microsoft, Amazon, Cohere, ByteDance, MiniMax, Zhipu AI, Moonshot AI and other research organisations.

How often is the AI model timeline updated?

The timeline is updated regularly as notable models are announced. The latest revision date appears below and in the public GitHub repository.
